Comprehensive Services

From floor plans, custom cabinetry, and interior and exterior design selections, to furnishings and everything in between, we offer clients a fully-integrated, all-inclusive, design+build experience that is efficient, well-organized and refreshingly smooth.

Custom Homes

We create original, one-of-a-kind, custom homes that are as spectacular as they are enduring.

From the fresh appeal of Michigan's lakefront living, to crisp, new, contemporary styling; from the traditional character of an estate home, to the eclectic feel of a transitional interior, our homes reflect some of the world's most celebrated styles.

Hire your own architect or work with one of our widely recognized designers to articulate your vision and create an original work-of-art.

Refreshing Remodels

Whether you are adding a second story, a new master suite, or a state-of-the-art kitchen, Mike Schaap Builders has deep expertise in large-scale renovation projects.

We've helped homeowners breathe new life into their existing homes, creating spectacular new spaces that deliver unmatched value and uncompromising quality.

You can rest assured knowing that these services are all completed by the same talent and craftsmanship that goes into our custom homes.

Design + Build 

Our design+build process harnesses the talents of our architectural, construction, and interior design teams from the beginning. 

From concept to completion, we remained focused on the design, constructibility and budget throughout all phases of the project. This approach ensures that ideas build upon one another, and designs progress with creative solutions and cost- and time-saving alternatives that ensure your vision. 

And while this approach is not only efficient, it has proven to deliver both an exceptional product and client experience.

External Partnerships

We have built strong relationships with some of the nation's most recognized architects, interior designers and trade specialists professionals through the caliber of our work, and by applying our expertise to help drive innovation, efficiency and cost savings on each project.

From design development to construction, we provide insight on real-time market costs, site conditions, schedule impact, lakefront building and protection strategies, sustainable technologies, and procurement.

Clients and their architects come to us because we listen carefully, share their vision, and bring designs to life in the most intricate detail.

Interior Design

The creativity and design expertise of our in-house interior design team, Benchmark Design Studio, comes as another valued benefit of Mike Schaap Builders.

Involved from the earliest phases of your design planning, our team listens closely to understand your priorities,  style preferences, and lifestyle and to assist you with not only the interior selections including floors, fixtures, appliances, finishes, carpets, window treatments, wall décor, textiles, decorative accents and accessories, but the exterior finished and your architectural design as well.

By encompassing the design in its many realms, the results are undeniably cohesive and custom.

Cabinetry & Millwork

A principal advantage of working with Mike Schaap Builders is the precise detail, customization, and ease that comes from our state-of-the-art in-house woodshop.

With over 50 highly skilled woodworkers dedicated to the craft of making every type of custom component imaginable, Benchmark Wood Studio works alongside Mike Schaap Builders and Benchmark Design Studio to deliver unparalleled custom cabinetry, millwork and furniture pieces.

Property Management

Every home requires year-round maintenance and Mike Schaap Builders has taken the unique step in offering property management services to our clients.

Whether your home is  a primary residence or a season one, you have peace-of-mind knowing that the same exacting care that went into finely crafting your home is now working to maintain its like-new condition.

This service of MSB Property Management, LLC provides clients with the opportunity to experience care-free living while maintaining their privacy and budget.
