# Font stand-ins

Use this table when the reference font is paid or cannot be licensed for the client.
Pick the free family with the closest x-height and width. Keep the reference size, weight, case, and line-height.
Two families maximum. Log the pick in `DESIGN.md`. Free reference fonts need no stand-in (logged clone exception).

| Reference font | Free stand-in | x-height | Width |
|---|---|---|---|
| Helvetica, Neue Haas Grotesk, Suisse Int'l, Söhne | Schibsted Grotesk | medium | normal |
| Neue Corp | Schibsted Grotesk | medium | normal |
| GT America, Aktiv Grotesk | Familjen Grotesk | medium | normal |
| Aeonik, Circular, Proxima Nova | Manrope | large | slightly wide |
| Avenir, Gill Sans | Nunito Sans | large | wide |
| Futura, Century Gothic | League Spartan | small | wide, geometric |
| Druk, Druk Wide, Compacta | Anton | small | condensed |
| Monument Grotesk | Space Grotesk | medium | normal |
| Clash Display, PP Neue Montreal | Syne | medium | wide display |
| Canela, Reckless, GT Super, Tiempos Headline | Fraunces | medium | normal, high contrast |
| Lyon, Tiempos Text, Mercury | Newsreader | medium | text serif |
| Garamond, Sabon | EB Garamond | small | old-style |
| Bodoni, Didot | Bodoni Moda | small | modern, high contrast |
| Editorial New, PP Editorial | Instrument Serif | small | sharp display |

A font that is not in the table still needs a free stand-in. Compare x-height and width with the rows above. Do not ship the paid file.
